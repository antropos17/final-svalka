## Economy and save contract

Independent enumeration confirms 64 equally likely outcomes: 4 triples, 36 exact pairs, 24 all-different; rewards are respectively 23, 8, and 3. Expected reward is **7.0625 coins/spin**, or **121.07 coins/minute** at an uninterrupted 3.5-second cycle. That rate excludes save/presentation delays, selection time, and upgrades; it is not an observed play rate.

A new save receives 37 coins once. Selecting the 40-coin repair and taking one ordinary spin leaves 0, 5, or 20 coins after purchase and unlocks AUTO. Selecting after the spin must also purchase once. With the unmodified base income, another 600-coin upgrade is roughly five minutes of continuous spinning; measure the distribution before setting first-session expectations. Cover exact funds, surplus, free preview, switching unpaid selection, and no automatically selected successor.

Save each transition as a complete snapshot and serialize writes. Keep a last validated backup, validate versions and sequence invariants, and preserve both candidates when recovery is uncertain. Two application-level writes are not one durable transaction. [Capacitor Preferences](https://capacitorjs.com/docs/apis/preferences) uses Android SharedPreferences; [Android apply](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/content/SharedPreferences.Editor#apply()) updates memory and writes asynchronously without reporting disk failures. Duplicate protection applies within the recovered snapshot; rollback can also roll back sequence history. Do not promise cross-rollback exactly-once durability or use this storage as a payment ledger. Revisit the adapter only when measured write cost or stronger requirements justify it.

## Verified external requirements

- **Closed testing:** for personal Play accounts created after 2023-11-13, at least 12 testers must remain continuously opted in for the preceding 14 days before applying for production access. Completing that period permits an application, not automatic approval. App setup precedes closed testing. A 16–20-person recruitment target is only a buffer. [Google Play testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en). Keep the same app/cohort while updating; continuity must be checked in Console. No account evidence was available here.
- **Owner verification:** new personal accounts may require physical-device verification through the owner’s Play Console mobile app; the documented device is non-rooted Android 10+. This is separate from testing this game's APK. [Google device verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14316361?hl=en).
- **Target API:** new phone apps and updates must target API 36 from 2026-08-31. Build for 36 rather than rely on an unverified extension. [Google Play target API requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878?hl=en). Check the final bundle and added SDKs for [16 KB compatibility](https://developer.android.com/guide/practices/page-sizes); the February 2027 update restriction is not a reason to defer compatibility work.
- **Stack:** [Phaser 4.2.1](https://phaser.io/download/release/v4.2.1) and [Capacitor 8.5.3](https://github.com/ionic-team/capacitor/releases/tag/8.5.3) are published releases. [Capacitor 8](https://capacitorjs.com/docs/updating/8-0) documents Node 22+, Android Studio Otter 2025.2.1+, minSdk 24 and compile/target 36. Current [Vite](https://vite.dev/guide/) requires Node 20.19+ or 22.12+; use a verified compatible Node 22.12+ or newer supported LTS, not any 22.x. Pin Vite/TypeScript and aligned Capacitor core/Android/CLI; plugin versions require their own compatibility checks. Published versions do not prove this machine or game works.
- **AdMob:** new apps require [app-ads.txt verification](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/14538460?hl=en-GB); [setup](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9363762?hl=en) requires the developer website in the store listing. Full serving also depends on [app readiness review and account status](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10564477?hl=en). [UMP guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy) requires refreshing consent information and checking `canRequestAds()` before requests. Verify current Capacitor plugin/native SDK compatibility and actual audience/regions before B4; test ads do not prove live eligibility or income.
