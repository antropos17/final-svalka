## Direct mapping to03's bounded candidates

**Construction600→540 or480:**03's current announced experiment uses eight reused seeds ×two existing policies; no request for another sweep. These are10%/20% local price reductions, not source-derived “correct” prices. Use the smallest candidate with a useful paired house-gap reduction; reject a larger discount whose only added merit is total chapter speed. If neither has a meaningful local advantage, keep600 and record that the hypothesis failed. A remaining long gap is not itself evidence of boredom. Only a subsequent unaided observation can test whether people want a break or disengage.

**Orders, reward-only:** do not compare “12 versus3” as equal units. Three completed deliveries require nine pigeon appearances from zero carry; existing0..2 carry and per-spin overshoot alter individual times. Trash requires12 appearances. Equal symbol probabilities do not erase that workload difference. Delivery skills increase coins per completed delivery, not event frequency. Actual Core paired counts supersede a naive occurrence-rate calculation.

Keep targets/cadence unchanged while comparing03's reward candidates; this permits arithmetic over existing results without rerunning the chapter. For current mean definitions, a candidate trash reward `R` rescales its extra claim rate by `R/120`; a delivery candidate `D` rescales its rate by `D/180`. Preserve each seed/policy aggregate rather than treating three sequential orders as independent samples. Claims include only the bonus; both order branches also retain their ordinary spin income. Pairwise efficiency need not match on every random sequence. Reward equality alone does not fix workload asymmetry, and per-event proportionality alone does not fully account for carry or interaction delay. Exact candidate values belong to03's bounded experiment; this research approves no table.

Current reward timing is correctness evidence: explicit acceptance, future events, explicit claim, one payout. New rewards need an explicit compatibility/version decision preserving existing accepted/ready/frozen amounts; never reprice a saved promise silently. That constraint follows our journal, not a competitor analogy.02 owns release/migration acceptance if a candidate is later adopted.

## Concrete decisions this review rejects

- Adopting skill Candidate A to solve the construction gap: the actual paired marginal gain is about23 nominal seconds for the chapter, with long gaps remaining.
- Declaring a universal90/120/165-second boredom threshold, or requiring parity because a forum dislikes waits. The sources and current receipts do not establish one.
- Changing reel probabilities, delivery cadence, prices and order rewards together: this loses attribution and exceeds the bounded question.
- Creating a mandatory postchapter sink or artificial attendance pressure to give coin rewards a purpose. Optional collection and a finite ending remain valid.
- Reopening r6 claims that every income offer is identical: current construction has19 symbol contributions and six skills. The current question is usefulness in reachable purchase windows, not the old two-project catalog.
