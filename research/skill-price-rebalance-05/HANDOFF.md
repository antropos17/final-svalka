## Search rule and numeric margin thresholds

The bounded binary search selects the highest tested round price on the declared grid for which every policy/conditioning has a best insertion delta<=-1.0 spin. Grid10 is used for Delivery1, Universal2 and Recycling2. Delivery2 uses grid5 after the first run established that even10 misses this chosen margin. The adjacent higher grid price is explicitly checked and fails the margin. Monotone price cost with fixed effects/prerequisites is the search assumption; this is a round-grid selection, not a claim of an exact highest integer break-even price.

Delta is expected spins **with** the inserted node minus the otherwise identical plan **without** it. Negative helps; positive favors saving. Tier2 deltas are incremental over its parent-conditioned plan, not over a plan with no parent.
